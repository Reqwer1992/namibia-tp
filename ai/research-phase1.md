## 1. Visa & Entry Requirements for Latvian Citizens

### Key Change (April 2025)
Namibia abolished visa-free entry for 33 countries, including Latvia, effective April 1, 2025. Latvian citizens can no longer enter visa-free.

### Current Options
1. **E-Visa (recommended)**: Apply online at [eservices.mhaiss.gov.na](https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/visaonarrival) before travel. Requires passport photo, scanned passport, and online payment. Processing reportedly fast (many approved within 24 hours of system launch).
2. **Visa on Arrival**: Available at entry points. Max stay 90 days. Cost: NAD 1,600 (~EUR 80 / USD 88).

### Passport Requirements
- Valid for **at least 6 months** beyond entry date
- **At least 3 blank pages** for stamps

### Documents Needed at Entry
- Valid passport (6+ months validity, 3+ blank pages)
- Return or onward flight ticket
- Proof of accommodation / travel itinerary
- Travel insurance documentation
- Visa approval (if e-visa obtained in advance)

### Travel Insurance
Medical and travel insurance is **mandatory** for the visa application.

## 2. Vaccinations & Health

### Required Vaccinations
- **Yellow fever**: Only required if arriving from or transiting (12+ hours) through a yellow-fever-endemic country. Latvia is NOT endemic, so **not required** for direct EU→Namibia travel.
- No other vaccinations are legally required for entry.

### Recommended Vaccinations
- Hepatitis A & B
- Typhoid
- Routine vaccinations up to date (tetanus, diphtheria, etc.)

### Malaria
- **Risk zones**: Northern Namibia — Etosha, Caprivi/Zambezi, Kavango regions
- **Seasonal**: Highest risk November–April (rainy season), low/zero risk May–October
- **Prophylaxis**: Malarone (atovaquone/proguanil) recommended for Etosha visitors. Doxycycline and mefloquine are alternatives.
- **2026 outbreak**: Namibia issued a malaria outbreak alert in early 2026 for northern/northeastern regions. Consult a travel health specialist before the trip.
- **Prevention**: Use DEET repellent, sleep under treated nets if possible, wear long sleeves at dusk/dawn.

### COVID-19
No COVID-19 entry requirements remain as of 2025.

## 3. British Airways Flights: LHR → Windhoek (WDH)

### Direct Flights
**No direct flights exist** from London Heathrow to Windhoek on any airline.

### BA Routing Options
All BA-bookable options require at least one connection:

| Route | Airlines | Approx. Duration |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| LHR → Johannesburg → WDH | BA + Airlink (codeshare) | ~14–16 hours |
| LHR → Cape Town → WDH | BA + Airlink (codeshare) | ~15–17 hours |
| LHR → Munich → WDH | BA + Lufthansa/Eurowings | ~13.5 hours |
| LHR → Frankfurt → WDH | BA/Lufthansa + Condor/Eurowings | ~15 hours |

### BA + Airlink Codeshare (Best Option)
- Since February 2023, BA has a codeshare partnership with **South African airline Airlink**.
- Airlink operates JNB → WDH and CPT → WDH routes.
- Bookable as a single ticket on ba.com.
- BA Executive Club members **earn Avios** on codeshare flights.
- This is the most practical option for a BA-focused itinerary.

### Approximate Pricing
- Return economy LHR → WDH: **GBP 650–1,200** depending on season and how far in advance you book.
- Booking 2–3 months ahead generally gives good prices.

### Airport in Namibia
- **Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH)** — 45 km east of Windhoek. This is where all international flights arrive.
- Eros Airport (ERS) is domestic only.

## 4. Summary & Recommendations

### Entry
- Apply for e-visa online before departure (NAD 1,600 / ~EUR 80)
- Ensure passports valid 6+ months, with 3+ blank pages
- Arrange travel insurance (mandatory)

### Flights
- Book via ba.com using the BA + Airlink codeshare via Johannesburg
- Budget GBP 700–1,000 per person return
- Book 2–3 months in advance

### Health
- Consult a travel clinic about malaria prophylaxis (Malarone recommended)
- No required vaccinations for direct EU travel, but Hep A/B and typhoid recommended
- Carry DEET repellent
