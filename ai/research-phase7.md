# Phase 7 Research: Driving License Requirements for Latvian Citizens in Namibia

## Key Finding

A Latvian driving license alone is **not legally sufficient** to drive in Namibia. Namibian law requires foreign licenses to be in English, or accompanied by an English translation/IDP.

## Namibian Law

Per the **Road Traffic and Transport Act, 1999 (Act No. 22 of 1999)** and Government Gazette (30 March 2001), a foreign driving license is accepted if:

1. It is **in English**, OR has an **English translation by a sworn translator**, OR has an **English certificate of authenticity** from a competent authority (embassy)
2. It contains a **photograph** of the holder
3. It contains the **signature** of the holder

The Latvian license is printed in Latvian (not English), so it requires either an IDP or sworn English translation.

## IDP Convention Mismatch

- **Namibia** is party to the **1949 Geneva Convention** (acceded 1993). NOT party to 1968 Vienna Convention.
- **Latvia** issues IDPs under the **1968 Vienna Convention** only (via CSDD).
- The UK Government states Namibia requires a "1949 convention IDP."

**In practice**: A 1968 IDP serves the same translation function and is accepted by Namibian police and rental companies. The booklet translates your license details into English and other languages, satisfying the underlying legal requirement.

## How to Get an IDP from CSDD (Latvia)

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Authority** | CSDD (Ceļu satiksmes drošības direkcija) |
| **Convention** | 1968 Vienna Convention |
| **Cost** | EUR 19.84 |
| **Processing** | 2–3 working days |
| **Validity** | 3 years (or until Latvian license expires, whichever is sooner) |
| **Apply** | In person at CSDD service centre, or remotely via e-CSDD with electronic signature |
| **Collect** | At CSDD centre or by post to Latvian address |
| **Languages** | Russian, English, Spanish, French |
| **Required** | Valid Latvian national driving license |

The IDP is only valid **together with** the original Latvian driving license. Not a standalone document.

Source: [csdd.lv/en/international-driving-permit-idp](https://www.csdd.lv/en/international-driving-permit-idp/general-information)

## Rental Company Requirements

- Most Namibian rental companies **require an IDP** if your license is not in English
- Minimum age typically 23 (some require 25; 21–25 may face surcharges)
- Must have held license for at least 1 year
- **Both drivers** must have their own license + IDP
- Confirm with your chosen rental company before departure

## Police Roadblocks

- Police roadblocks are **common** throughout Namibia — both near cities and on main roads
- Officers typically ask for driving license, vehicle registration, insurance documents
- For non-English licenses, the IDP is commonly requested
- Having an IDP ready speeds up the process and eliminates risk of fines

## Penalties

- Driving without valid license: fine **NAD 500–3,000** (~EUR 25–150)
- Possible imprisonment 6–18 months
- Vehicle may be seized
- Driving with a non-English license without IDP/translation may be treated as driving without a valid license

## Recommendations

1. **Get the Latvian 1968 IDP from CSDD** — EUR 19.84, 2–3 working days. Practically accepted everywhere.
2. **Both drivers need their own IDP** — each person who will drive needs their own.
3. **Always carry both documents** — original Latvian license + IDP together.
4. **Confirm with rental company** before departure that they accept Latvian license + IDP.
5. **Keep documents accessible** in the vehicle for police roadblocks.
