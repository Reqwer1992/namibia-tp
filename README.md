## Entry Requirements

### Visa (Latvian / EU Citizens)

As of April 2025, Latvia is no longer visa-free for Namibia. You need a visa.

**Option 1 — E-Visa (recommended)**
- Apply online before departure at [eservices.mhaiss.gov.na](https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/visaonarrival)
- Cost: **NAD 1,600 (~EUR 80 / ~USD 88)** per person
- Processing: reportedly fast (often approved within 24 hours)
- Requires: passport photo upload, scanned passport, online payment

**Option 2 — Visa on Arrival**
- Available at Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH)
- Same cost: NAD 1,600 per person
- Max stay: 90 days
- Risk of queues; e-visa is safer

### Passport Requirements

- Valid for **at least 6 months** beyond date of entry
- **At least 3 blank pages** for entry/exit stamps

### Documents Checklist

- [ ] Valid passport (6+ months validity, 3+ blank pages)
- [ ] E-visa approval (apply in advance)
- [ ] Return flight ticket (or onward travel proof)
- [ ] Proof of accommodation / travel itinerary
- [ ] Travel insurance (mandatory for visa) — must cover medical and travel-related issues
- [ ] Printed copies of all documents (don't rely solely on phone)

### Vaccinations & Health

**Required vaccinations**: None for direct travel from the EU. Yellow fever certificate only needed if transiting 12+ hours through an endemic country.

**Recommended vaccinations**:
- Hepatitis A & B
- Typhoid
- Routine vaccines up to date (tetanus, diphtheria, polio)

**Malaria**:
- Risk exists in **northern Namibia** (Etosha, Caprivi/Zambezi region), especially November–April (rainy season)
- **Prophylaxis recommended**: Malarone (atovaquone/proguanil) is the standard choice. Alternatives: doxycycline, mefloquine.
- Carry **DEET-based insect repellent** (30–50% concentration)
- Wear long sleeves and trousers at dusk and dawn
- Consult a travel health clinic **6–8 weeks before departure**

**COVID-19**: No entry requirements remain as of 2025.

## Flight Options

### Route: London Heathrow (LHR) → Windhoek (WDH)

There are **no direct flights** from London to Windhoek. All options require at least one connection.

### Recommended: BA + Airlink Codeshare via Johannesburg

British Airways has a **codeshare partnership with Airlink** (since Feb 2023). This is the best option for flying BA:

- **Route**: LHR → Johannesburg (JNB) → Windhoek (WDH)
- **Operated by**: BA (LHR→JNB) + Airlink (JNB→WDH)
- **Total duration**: ~14–16 hours (including layover)
- **Bookable**: Single ticket on [ba.com](https://www.britishairways.com)
- **Avios**: BA Executive Club members earn Avios on the full journey
- **Frequency**: Daily BA flights to JNB; multiple daily Airlink connections JNB→WDH

### Alternative Routes

| Route | Via | Approx. Duration | Airlines |
|-------|-----|-------------------|----------|
| LHR → JNB → WDH | Johannesburg | 14–16 hrs | BA + Airlink |
| LHR → CPT → WDH | Cape Town | 15–17 hrs | BA + Airlink |
| LHR → MUC → WDH | Munich | ~13.5 hrs | BA + Lufthansa/Eurowings |
| LHR → FRA → WDH | Frankfurt | ~15 hrs | Various combinations |

### Pricing

- **Return economy**: approximately **GBP 650–1,200** per person
- Best prices when booked **2–3 months in advance**
- Peak season (June–October, Namibia's dry season / best wildlife viewing) tends to be more expensive

### Arrival Airport

**Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH)** — located 45 km east of Windhoek city centre. All international flights arrive here. Vehicle rental companies typically offer airport pickup/drop-off.
